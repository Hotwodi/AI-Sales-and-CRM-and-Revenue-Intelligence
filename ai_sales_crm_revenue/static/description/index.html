AI Sales, CRM & Revenue Intelligence

Transform your sales process with AI-driven scoring, forecasting, and customer insights

AI Sales, CRM & Revenue Intelligence is a comprehensive Odoo 18 module that brings the power of artificial intelligence to your sales and revenue operations. From lead scoring to churn prediction, this module provides actionable insights that help your team close more deals, forecast accurately, and retain valuable customers.

AI Lead Scoring

  • Automated lead scoring (0-100)
  • Conversion probability estimation
  • AI recommendations: Pursue, Nurture, or Disqualify
  • Transparent score factor breakdown

Revenue Forecasting

  • Conservative, Base, and Optimistic scenarios
  • AI confidence levels per forecast
  • Automatic variance calculation
  • Product category segmentation

Customer Insights

  • Lifetime Value (LTV) tracking
  • AI segmentation: VIP, Growth, Stable, At Risk
  • Engagement and churn risk scoring
  • AI-recommended actions per customer

Pipeline Analytics

  • Total and weighted pipeline views
  • Win rate and sales cycle metrics
  • AI-powered revenue forecasts
  • Team-level performance breakdown

Churn Prediction

  • Churn probability scoring
  • Risk factor identification
  • State tracking: Active, At Risk, Churned, Saved
  • Retention action recommendations
